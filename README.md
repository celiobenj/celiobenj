Computer Engineering student at the **State University of Amazonas (UEA)**. Embedded Systems Leader at [**GEAR (Grupo de Estudos Aplicados a Robotica)**](https://github.com/GEAR-EST) and developer/admin at [**STEM Criar**](https://github.com/stemcriar). 

Specialized in low-level firmware engineering, competition robotics, Industrial IoT (Industry 4.0), and hardware-software integration.
